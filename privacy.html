Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Nav handles information.

Information Collection

Nav does not require user registration or account creation.

Nav does not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Physical address
  • Payment information

Local Files

Nav is a local file explorer application.

Files selected by the user remain on the user's device, storage system, or network storage.

Nav does not upload user files to the developer's servers.

Network Access

Certain features may access network resources specified by the user, such as local network storage, media resources, or web content.

These requests are initiated by the user and are performed solely to provide requested functionality.

Analytics

Nav does not use third-party analytics services.

Nav does not track user behavior across apps or websites.

Advertising

Nav does not display advertisements.

Data Sharing

Nav does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

Data Retention

Any settings, preferences, favorites, or application data are stored locally on the user's device unless otherwise specified by the user.

Children's Privacy

Nav is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

Security

Reasonable efforts are made to protect application data stored locally on the user's device.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.
